Meet the Penguin 🐧

An introduction to nature's aquatic birds

What is a Penguin?

Penguins are flightless seabirds that live almost exclusively in the Southern Hemisphere. While they cannot fly in the air, their wings have evolved into flippers, making them expert swimmers under water!

Fun Penguin Facts

  • Heavy Swimmers: Penguins can spend up to 75% of their lives in the ocean.
  • Natural Tuxedo: Their black and white coloring acts as camouflage called countershading.
  • Warm Blood: They have a thick layer of fat (blubber) and dense feathers to survive freezing temperatures.
